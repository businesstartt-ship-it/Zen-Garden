Our Story

Where the city breathes and the garden speaks

Zen Garden by DeshaTarc is the first ever nature-embraced serene rooftop restaurant in Kushtia. We created a sanctuary above the bustle — a place where lush greenery, soft breezes, and thoughtful cuisine invite you to slow down and savour every moment.

From sizzling beef steaks to vibrant vegan plates, every dish is served under open skies surrounded by living plants. It's not just a meal — it's a breath of calm.
